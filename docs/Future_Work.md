# Future Work

The immediate priority is preservation and completion of the UBCF archive. Formal publication work should follow only after the archive has been populated and checked for internal consistency.

## Archive Completion
- Capture all established definitions.
- Preserve development examples and analogies.
- Record open questions without forcing answers.
- Preserve speculative extensions separately from core framework rules.
- Maintain design decisions and revision history.

## Consistency Review
- Compare definitions across documents.
- Identify contradictions and ambiguous terminology.
- Separate assumptions from consequences.
- Verify that time-travel rules follow ordinary UBCF branching rules without special exceptions where possible.

## Formalization
- Develop mathematical representations of events, branches, and Causal Lineage.
- Explore branching factor, causal depth, distinguishability, and information history.
- Determine whether continuous outcome spaces require a different representation than discrete branch diagrams.

## Physics and Philosophy Research
- Compare UBCF carefully with Many-Worlds interpretations, causal-set approaches, branching-time logic, modal realism, quantum decoherence, cosmological natural selection, and black-hole/baby-universe hypotheses.
- Identify similarities without claiming equivalence.

## Testability
- Investigate whether UBCF can generate predictions or constraints distinguishable from existing models.

## Publication
After archive completion and consistency review, prepare a formal paper derived from the canonical repository rather than treating the paper itself as the primary record.
