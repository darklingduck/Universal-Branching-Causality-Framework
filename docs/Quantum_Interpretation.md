# Quantum Interpretation

## Status
Exploratory application of UBCF, not a replacement for quantum mechanics.

## Collapse and Branching
A collapse interpretation selects one realized outcome while alternatives no longer describe the observed state. A branching interpretation instead allows distinguishable outcomes to continue in separate histories.

UBCF is naturally interested in the second structure because an Event Branch Node represents divergence into distinguishable causal continuations.

## Uncertainty
Quantum uncertainty may provide a physical possibility space from which distinguishable causal outcomes arise. UBCF does not currently provide a mathematical quantum model and does not claim that every quantum interpretation must branch.

## Probability
Probability is not used in UBCF to decide which normal branch is real. Normal branching produces causal continuations rather than a winning branch.

Probability has a special role in temporal displacement when a traveler skips the intermediate causal events that would ordinarily connect one experienced point to another.

## Open Work
The relationship among quantum state evolution, distinguishability, Event Branch Nodes, and UBCF requires future mathematical and physical analysis.
