# Information and Reality

## Status
Working archive material. Capture first; refinement later.

## Core Idea
A branch can be understood as an accumulated causal information history. The events and choices in its Causal Lineage determine what that branch has become.

A useful UBCF analogy is: **choices are the DNA of a branch.** Just as biological information influences the development of an organism, accumulated causal events determine the development of a branch.

## Shared Information Before Divergence
When an Event Branch Node occurs, the new branches do not each recreate a separate history back to the beginning of reality. They share one Causal Lineage up to the branch point. Information differs only from the point of divergence forward.

## New Information After Divergence
Each distinguishable outcome introduces a different causal continuation. From that point onward, the information history of each branch develops independently.

## Identity
The Identity Continuity Structure is affected by the causal information in its branch. Biological, environmental, social, and physical events all contribute to what an observer becomes.

## Limits
UBCF does not currently claim that information is more fundamental than matter or energy. The framework uses information as a way to describe accumulated causal history.
