# Time and Causality

## Time Within UBCF

UBCF describes time as the ordering structure of causal events.

## Limits of Observation

An observer inside time cannot determine what exists outside time because leaving time would either require entering another temporal structure or experiencing a condition that cannot be observed from within the system.

## Before Time

Questions about what existed before time remain open, similar to questions about conditions before the Big Bang.

## Causality

Branches develop from causal relationships. Time provides the ordering through which Causal Lineage is formed.
