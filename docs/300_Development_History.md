# Development History

## Purpose
This document preserves the intellectual path that led to UBCF. It is historical context, not evidence that the framework is physically correct.

## 1. Religion, Scripture, and Authority
The original discussion began with questions about scripture, Jewish monotheism, Christian Trinitarian theology, religious authority, and whether later institutional doctrine represented continuity or change.

## 2. Skepticism and Agnosticism
The discussion expanded into institutional power, the historical development of religions, comparative reading of the Tanakh, Christian Bible, Qur'an, and Satanic Bible, and a position of critical or historically informed agnosticism.

## 3. Free Will and Guidance
Questions about divine guidance led to a broader examination of free will, determinism, randomness, upbringing, biology, and environmental influence.

## 4. Neutral Identity and Moral Branching
A working idea emerged that a soul or identity need not begin as inherently good or evil. Different causal paths could lead versions of an individual toward different moral outcomes while the broader identity concept remained neutral.

## 5. Universal Branching
Branching was expanded beyond personal choice. A causal event anywhere in reality could produce different complete histories, including events entirely unrelated to a particular observer.

## 6. Nature, Nurture, and Causal History
Sensory experience, environment, family, trauma, support, biology, and other interactions were recognized as parts of the Causal Lineage that shape identity.

## 7. Time, Cosmology, and Evolution
The framework expanded to include the Big Bang, creation as a possible prior cause rather than necessarily a competing explanation, evolution, and the possibility that even a creation event could have a prior cause.

## 8. Time Travel
Backward, forward, and sideways temporal displacement were explored. Branching at departure and arrival became central rules, resolving classic paradoxes without altering an originating branch.

## 9. Identity Continuity Structure
The Identity Continuity Structure (ICS) became the preferred model for observer identity. Immediately before divergence, resulting continuations are exact copies; after the Event Branch Node, each experiences its own causal continuation.

## 10. Event Branch Nodes and Causal Lineage
The framework moved away from human-centered 'choice.' Any causal event with multiple distinguishable outcomes may be an Event Branch Node. Causal Lineage became the accumulated event history defining a branch.

## 11. Quantum and Cosmological Extensions
Quantum uncertainty, collapse versus branching, black-hole cosmology, nested universes, and differing fundamental laws were explored as possible applications rather than settled core axioms.

## 12. Universal Branching Causality Framework
The name Universal Branching Causality Framework (UBCF) was adopted, with Identity Continuity Structure (ICS) replacing earlier soul-centered language for the formal framework.

## 13. Archive Development
The GitHub repository was established as the canonical living archive. The immediate priority became preserving the complete framework, examples, unresolved questions, and development history before returning to a formal paper.
