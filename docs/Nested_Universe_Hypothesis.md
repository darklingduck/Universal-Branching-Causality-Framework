# Nested Universe Hypothesis

## Status
Speculative hypothesis permitted by UBCF; not established as a core axiom.

## Concept
A universe may potentially exist within a larger cosmological structure. One explored possibility is that black holes in a parent universe could be associated with the formation of child universes.

Conceptually:

Parent or Macroverse
→ Black Hole / Cosmological Transition
→ Child Universe

## Physical Laws
UBCF does not currently require all branches or nested universes to possess identical fundamental laws. A sufficiently early or cosmic-scale divergence might produce conditions that are fundamentally different from those familiar to observers in our branch.

This possibility is deliberately left open rather than asserted as fact.

## Observational Limit
There is currently no UBCF claim that our universe has been demonstrated to exist inside a black hole. The idea is retained as a cosmological extension and research question.
