# Cosmological Applications

## Status
Exploratory application of the framework.

## Universal Branching
UBCF is not Earth-centered or observer-centered. Event Branch Nodes may occur anywhere causal events have multiple distinguishable outcomes, including events that no human observer will ever know occurred.

Examples include stellar events, meteor interactions, planetary development, biological evolution, and other remote causal processes.

## Big Bang, Creation, and Evolution
UBCF does not require the Big Bang, creation, and evolution to be mutually exclusive ideas. A creator, prior cause, or unknown origin could in principle precede a cosmological beginning, while evolution could describe later development.

The framework does not assert which origin account is correct.

## Before the Beginning
Questions such as what existed before the Big Bang, whether time had a beginning, or whether creation itself was caused by an earlier event remain epistemically open.

## Different Cosmic Outcomes
Different early causal conditions could lead to radically different later universes, life, environments, and observers. UBCF treats such differences as potential consequences of divergent Causal Lineages.
