# Assumptions and Scope

## Purpose
This document separates what UBCF currently assumes, what it allows, and what it does not claim to know.

## Current Framework Assumptions
- Causal relationships can order events.
- Events can have multiple distinguishable outcomes.
- Distinguishable causal outcomes can produce independent branches.
- A branch has a Causal Lineage consisting of the events leading to its state.
- Identity continuity can be described using the Identity Continuity Structure.

## Not Required by UBCF
UBCF does not currently require:
- a specific religion or absence of religion;
- a particular origin of existence;
- a complete theory of consciousness;
- identical physical laws across every possible branch or universe;
- time travel to be physically achievable;
- the Nested Universe Hypothesis to be true.

## Epistemic Limits
UBCF deliberately leaves questions unanswered when an observer within the framework has no established way to obtain the required information. Examples include the fundamental nature of time outside experienced causality, what preceded the earliest describable event, and what happens to awareness after death.

## Current Scope
The present archive focuses on branching causality, Causal Lineage, Event Branch Nodes, Identity Continuity Structure, observer continuity, time, temporal displacement, information history, and speculative cosmological and quantum applications.
