# Observer Continuity Problem

## Definition

The Observer Continuity Problem describes how awareness continues through a branching reality.

## Continuity Before Branching

Immediately before an Event Branch Node, the Identity Continuity Structure is identical across all possible outcomes.

## Continuity After Branching

After the causal event occurs, each resulting ICS experiences only its own branch.

The observer does not experience every possible continuation. It experiences the continuation created by the causal history of that branch.

## Open Question

The framework describes the structure of continuity but does not claim to fully explain the fundamental nature of consciousness.
