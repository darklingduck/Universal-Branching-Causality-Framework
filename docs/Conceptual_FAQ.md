# UBCF Conceptual FAQ

## Does UBCF say only conscious choices create branches?
No. Any causal event with multiple distinguishable outcomes may produce an Event Branch Node.

## Does merely thinking about two choices create two physical branches?
Not by itself. The considered choice must enter the physical Causal Lineage through a causal change.

## Are Event Branch Nodes binary?
No. One EBN may have any number of distinguishable outcomes. A sequence of decisions is represented by multiple EBNs.

## Does one branch get selected as the real branch?
Not in ordinary UBCF branching. The framework does not use probability to choose a single real outcome from normal branch formation.

## Can two branches merge later?
No under the current Branch Independence Principle. Their post-divergence Causal Lineages remain different even if later states look alike.

## Does a new branch copy the entire universe back to creation?
No. The pre-divergence history is shared. The new causal histories differ from the EBN forward.

## Can a time traveler return to the exact branch they left?
No under the current model. Departure changes causality, and any later arrival is another causal event that creates a new branch.

## Does forward time travel guarantee a compatible future?
No. A traveler may arrive in any future produced by the skipped causal structure, including futures radically unlike the one expected.

## Does UBCF prove black holes create universes?
No. Nested universes are retained as a speculative application.

## Does UBCF explain what happens after death?
No. The framework treats this as an epistemic boundary rather than asserting an answer.
