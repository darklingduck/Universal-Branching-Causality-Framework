# Related Theories and Researchers

## Status
Historical research notes; not current UBCF canon and not evidence of endorsement.

## Purpose
This file preserves the external concepts and researchers explored while trying to understand where UBCF overlaps with existing scientific and philosophical work.

## Related Areas
Historical comparison topics included:

- Everett-style Many-Worlds interpretations,
- quantum branching and wavefunction collapse,
- black-hole cosmology,
- baby-universe hypotheses,
- cosmological natural selection,
- causal structure and time in gravitation,
- quantum gravity, and
- speculative nested-universe models.
